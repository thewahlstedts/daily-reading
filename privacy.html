Privacy

Daily Reading is a free, non-commercial app for reading through the Bible with a group. It has no ads and no tracking, and nothing about you is sold or shared for marketing. This page explains what it keeps and why. Last updated November 11, 2026.

Without an account

Your reading progress, marked verses, notes and settings are saved only on your device. Signing in is optional.

If you sign in

You sign in with your email address. To sync between your devices, the app stores on its server your email address, reading progress (start date, meeting day and which readings you've checked off), marked verses with any notes you write on them, and your choices of translation and AI tool. Earlier copies of this are kept for up to 30 days so a mistake can be undone. Only you can read your data. Theme, voice and reading-text settings stay on each device.

Reminders

In the iPhone app, reminders are scheduled on your phone and nothing about them is sent anywhere. On the website, reminders need sign-in: the server stores your device's push address, the time you picked and your time zone, and uses your synced progress to name the day's reading.

Usage counts

Once a day, each device sends a random ID (created on the device, not linked to your phone or browser), whether it's the installed app, and, if you're signed in, your account. This is only used to count how many people use the app. Your reading, notes and IP address aren't part of it. These records are kept for about 400 days.

Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings, your message is emailed to the app's maintainer along with your email address (so they can reply), your translation, meeting day, and device and browser type.

Services the app uses

  • Supabase stores account data and runs sign-in.
  • Resend sends sign-in codes and feedback emails.
  • Bible text, commentary and audio come from the Free Use Bible API and Cloudflare. NIV, NLT and AMP come from API.Bible, which counts anonymous usage for the publishers' licensing. These services see which passage is requested, not who you are.
  • "Ask" links open the AI tool you chose (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Google) with a question naming the passage. Only the reference and translation name are sent, never your notes. What happens there is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

Like any website, these services keep standard request logs (which include IP addresses) for a short time.

Deleting your data

Signing out leaves your data on the device; clearing the app's data or deleting the app removes it. To delete your account and everything stored with it, send a request through Settings → Feedback and it will be removed.

Children

The app is meant for adults and families reading together, and doesn't knowingly collect anything from children beyond what's described here.

Questions

Use Settings → Feedback in the app.
